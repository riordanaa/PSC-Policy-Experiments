\section{The four findings that matter}

\textbf{F1 --- The binding constraint is information, not intelligence.} At thesis severity
the surviving manufacturer has 3.3$\times$ headroom and ramps with orders; a two-line
reroute on the \emph{shared upstream machine-state} signal exactly reproduces perfect onset
knowledge. Without that sharing, NO downstream policy --- learned or not --- can act in
time, because the dead distributor's own stock masks the failure for $\sim$6 periods.
\emph{Meaning: the thesis's information-sharing narrative is right, but its value is
captured by a trivial rule, not by a learner.}

\textbf{F2 --- The original hypothesis, half-confirmed, hinging on one missing file.}
From the distributor's seat, in the thesis's own world and metric, a 3-parameter compound
(serve-the-captive $\times$ taper $\times$ standing buffer) removes 49--51\% of base
stock's loss --- against the RL's claimed 89\% from a table we cannot replicate (our own
corrected-pipeline DRL \emph{lost} to base stock). \emph{Meaning: simple rules beat base
stock decisively; whether the RL's remaining margin is real is now a question about the
thesis baseline configuration --- the single most valuable item to obtain.}

\textbf{F3 --- Under scarcity, structure wins: location and sizing, not reaction.}
The frontier is owned by one composition; the buffer's optimal \emph{location} flips to the
disrupted chain when the surviving chain is weak; and demand-aware \emph{sizing} cuts lost
patients from 505 to 41. \emph{Meaning: the two deployable decisions that matter are where
stock sits and how it is sized --- both computable from observables, no learning required.}

\textbf{F4 --- Cleverness consistently loses to structure.} Across three studies, every
reactive ``smart'' rule backfired: delivery-rate detection, backlog-priority, serve-captive
(in the routing-fixed world), rotating priority, priority-with-floor, and both severity-aware
redirect caps. The trust feedback loop rewards consistency; the production loop is driven by
orders. \emph{Meaning: for the eventual RL comparison, the honest prior is that adaptivity
must overcome a measured pattern of adaptive rules underperforming static structure.}

\section{Phase-by-phase detail}

\subsection{Background: the DRL arc (why there is no working RL comparator)}
The reward audit found r1/r4 carrying a thesis-level algebraic defect, r5 a code defect,
r2/r3/r6 saturated as configured; repairs un-stuck all six components. Training instability
traced to the adaptive exploration rule (multiplicative runaway to its cap); a linear
schedule yields a stably-training agent --- which then \emph{loses} to base stock on
system profit ($-$4.06M vs $-$2.35M, driven by manufacturer queue cost). Table 3.9 is
therefore treated as a claim, not a reproduced result, pending the author's configuration.

\subsection{Routing study (thesis severity)}
HC2's hard-wired equal-split plus stranded-order suppression lock $\sim$2/3 of the
disruption cost into the routing layer: trust-split $-$26\%, sharpened trust $+$ stale-order
write-off $-$67\% (fill 1.000), oracle onset reroute $-$91\%; lost patients 1,768 $\to$ 203.
Found en route: the normal demand model hard-reseeds the global RNG (all historical
multi-seed normal-demand results were one path) --- fixed in our runners, gate-guarded.

\subsection{Understanding study (routing-fixed world)}
Allocation bounded at $\le$12\% via max-over-family (static priority; fairness cost
$\pm$0.023 fill); all dynamic rules backfire through the trust loop. The rung-c residual is
41\% dead-factory queue bookkeeping (dual accounting introduced for exactly this), the
$\sim$10-period redirection transient, and a write-off glut later removed as a side effect
of instant rerouting.

\subsection{Value decomposition, Phase 1 (slack)}
Info-sharing reroute $\equiv$ oracle (bit-identical, audited); explicit delivery-rate
detection worse than the built-in trust mechanism (stock-cover masking $\sim$0.9M);
deployable compound 273k. Verification rebuilt the oracle as a true superset and corrected
two claims: foresight is worth $\sim$54k with the buffer at the SURVIVING chain, and
slack buffers are negative only at the disrupted location ($+$4--7\% at the survivor).

\subsection{Value decomposition, Phase 2 (saturated regimes)}
With the surviving manufacturer also cut, routing still reduces cost ($-$26\%/$-$50\%) but
cannot create product (lost patients $\sim$flat across the entire ladder); the compound
reaches $-$79\% at fill 0.99--1.00; static priority flips harmful ($+$23\%); the
severity$\times$duration map has two boundaries (stock-cover duration; surviving-chain
headroom), with sharp rerouting the WORST measured policy at severe scarcity.

\subsection{DS-seat study (thesis world) and Part C}
Full-grid ladder over the distributor's complete decision space (everything else is dead by
topology). Results in F2/F4 and the claims table. Part C: demand-aware sizing (the robust
knob), the sat30 location correction, and the redirect-cap refutation with its mechanism
(orders are the production signal).

\section{Superseded claims (do not cite)}
\begin{itemize}
\item The $-$468k / $-$654k value split --- measured on the broken routing layer; retracted.
\item ``Permanent insurance captures 42\%'' --- broken-routing artifact; in the fixed-routing
slack world, disrupted-located buffers are zero-to-negative (surviving-located: $+$4--7\%).
\item ``Foresight adds nothing beyond the shared signal'' --- artifact of a mis-located
ceiling buffer; corrected to $\sim$54k ($\sim$5\%).
\item ``Detection captures $\approx$0'' --- true only for detect-then-buffer; rerouting
responses carry most of the onset value, and the upstream-signal trigger captures it fully.
\item ``Allocation $\le$12\% is the whole channel'' --- rung-c-specific; in the thesis world
the demand-shaping channel is worth $+$21.8\% (shed) and the compound 49\%.
\item ``Fill 1.000'' for the B960 scarcity compound --- display-rounding; 0.990 (B1440
reaches 0.9999).
\item The frozen per-severity buffer grids --- replaced by demand-aware sizing.
\end{itemize}

\section{Rigor record (one paragraph)}
All experiments ran on a gated harness (bit-exact baseline reproduction on both library and
CLI paths, determinism, demand conservation, cross-seed variance), with pre-registered
tuning/reporting seed splits (1--10 / 11--30), dual cost accounting, and both fairness
baselines carried. Every phase was audited by an independent agent re-deriving headline
numbers from raw CSVs (12/12, 11/12 --- the exception a display-rounding over-claim,
corrected --- 16/16, 13/13). Three contaminated or buggy batches were caught by gates or
diagnostics (CLI-default contamination; a bootstrap deadlock; both logged) and discarded
without contaminating conclusions. Simulator defects found and guarded: the normal demand
model's global RNG reseed, a dead trust-delta config constant, the silent discard of
orders $\le$1 unit, and the missing on-order timeout that produces stranded-pipeline
suppression.
