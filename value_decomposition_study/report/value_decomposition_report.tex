\section*{Summary}
We completed and stress-tested the value decomposition across two regimes: the thesis
scenario (one manufacturer at 5\% for 48 periods --- ``slack,'' because the surviving chain
has headroom) and a documented saturated extension (the surviving manufacturer also cut).
Four results. \textbf{(1) In the slack regime the binding constraint is information, not
intelligence:} a two-line reroute rule triggered on the \emph{shared upstream machine-state
signal} exactly reproduces the perfect-foresight oracle (bit-identical trajectories), cutting
cost from 1.06M to 295k and lost patients from 445 to 203; without that shared signal,
\emph{no} downstream policy of any sophistication can act in time, because the disrupted
distributor's own stock masks the failure from downstream observers for $\sim$6 periods
($\sim$0.9M of value). The measured room left for any learned policy above the deployable
compound is $\approx$0. \textbf{(2) Under scarcity the frontier becomes real and is owned by
one composition:} a standing buffer \emph{at the surviving chain} + the info-sharing reroute
+ an order taper achieves $-79\%$ cost at fill 0.990 --- 1.000 with the larger buffer ---
(urgent0) and $-68\%$ lost patients (urgent20); the parts alone achieve less than half of
that and fill $\le$ 0.99. \textbf{(3) The
dominant lever flips along two boundaries} --- no action (disruptions shorter than the
stock-cover window) $\to$ routing (slack) $\to$ compound (mild scarcity) $\to$ buffer-only
(severe scarcity, where sharp rerouting becomes the \emph{worst} measured policy).
\textbf{(4) Every ``clever'' reactive rule we tested} (delivery-rate detection,
backlog-chasing or rotating allocation, accounting fixes for the glut) \textbf{lost to a
simpler alternative}, and three prior headline numbers (the $-468$k/$-654$k decomposition,
the ``insurance captures 42\%'' result, and static-priority allocation's 12\% gain) were
shown to be artifacts of regime or of the broken routing layer. All gates passed; both
phases were independently audited by an agent re-deriving every number from raw CSVs
(Phase 1: 12/12 PASS; Phase 2: 11/12, the exception being a display-rounding over-statement
of one fill rate, corrected throughout this report).

\textbf{The information result.} The upstream-signal reroute --- flag a source while its
feeding manufacturer is below 50\% of nominal lines (exactly the visibility the thesis's
full information-sharing scenario grants), step the order split away from it --- reproduces
the perfect-foresight oracle \emph{bit-for-bit} (independently audited). A delivery-rate
detector, by contrast, is structurally blind for $\sim$6 periods (the dead distributor
serves from its own stock, so its delivery statistics look healthy exactly while the
pile-up begins) and ends up \emph{worse} than the built-in trust mechanism, which is itself
such a detector. The $\sim$750k between ``no sharing'' and ``sharing'' is therefore an
\emph{information} value, not an intelligence value --- no downstream policy, learned or
not, can close it. \textbf{This reframes the thesis's information-sharing narrative: the
value of sharing is real, large, and capturable by a two-line rule.}

\textbf{The deployable compound now IS the measured ceiling.} Adding the order taper
(a discrete rule on the same shared signal: while your manufacturer is down, order only what
it delivers) the deployable compound (273k) \emph{beats} every oracle-assisted composition
we built (284k+) --- foresight adds nothing beyond the shared signal. Dual accounting keeps
the taper honest: its gain is almost entirely dead-factory queue bookkeeping (ex-MN it is
$\approx$neutral), and it does not move patients.

\textbf{Negative results (all gated, all audited).} Standing buffers in the slack regime
have zero-to-\emph{negative} coverage at every size (a buffered \dsd{} feeds the dead
factory's queue and the recovery glut) --- this supersedes the earlier ``permanent insurance
captures 42\%'' finding, which was an artifact of the broken routing layer. Accounting-side
glut fixes fail by construction (the double-order is committed mid-window); instant
rerouting removes the glut as a side effect (peak excess 478 vs 2,967). Fairness-neutral
variants of priority allocation fail: rotating priority is catastrophic ($+$53\%, lost 740)
--- the trust feedback rewards \emph{consistency}; there is no fairness-free priority.

\section{Phase 2 --- scarcity: the frontier becomes real}

When the surviving manufacturer is also cut to 50\% (supply 220 vs demand 240/period),
routing still reduces cost ($-26\%$ deployable, $-50\%$ oracle) but \emph{cannot create
product}: fill pins at $\sim$0.9 and lost patients barely move across the entire routing
ladder (1,768 $\to$ $\sim$1,600; in slack the same ladder achieved 203). The residual is
inventory's domain:

Three structural findings from the screen: buffer \textbf{location} is first-order
(at the surviving chain $\gg$ split $\gg$ at the disrupted chain, which is near-worthless);
ordering shapers alone do nothing (supply-bound); and \textbf{static-priority allocation
flips harmful under scarcity} ($+$23\% vs proportional) --- starving one health center at
the queue-bound surviving distributor collapses its trust in the \emph{healthy} chain and
routes its orders toward the dead one. The slack-regime allocation gain does not transfer.

\section{What we learned}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The decomposition, completed:} value under unforecastable disruption =
information sharing (the largest single item in the slack regime, $\sim$750k, capturable by
a two-line rule) $+$ routing flexibility (dominant while the surviving chain has headroom)
$+$ correctly-located inventory (necessary exactly when it doesn't) $-$ over-reaction
(responses to sub-stock-cover disruptions; sharp rerouting under severe scarcity; clever
reactive allocation everywhere).
\item \textbf{The room for learned policies is $\approx$0 in the slack regime} (the
deployable compound matches/beats every oracle composition) \textbf{and not visible under
scarcity} (no measured policy beats the simple compound; the space closed under
screen-and-compose --- VNS was, per protocol, not justified: there is no residual gap for it
to search).
\item \textbf{Reactive cleverness consistently backfires} via the trust feedback loop:
delivery-rate detection, backlog-priority, serve-captive, rotating priority, and (under
scarcity) static priority all lose to simpler alternatives. Consistency and correct
\emph{location} beat adaptivity in every measured case.
\item \textbf{Three prior numbers are formally superseded:} the $-468$k/$-654$k
decomposition and the 42\%-insurance result (artifacts of the broken routing layer), and
allocation's 12\% gain (regime-specific, sign-flips under scarcity).
\item \textbf{Methodological:} the dual-accounting amendment caught a lever (the taper)
whose value is bookkeeping; the audit protocol (independent re-derivation) passed both
phases; two latent simulator bugs from earlier studies (RNG reseed, dead config) remained
guarded by gates throughout.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Next steps}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Severity-aware redirect} (new policy class --- awaiting your approval, spec in
\texttt{NEXT\_PLAN.md}): cap rerouted volume at the surviving chain's observed headroom.
Hypothesis: removes the only failure mode of the compound (over-redirection at severe
scarcity), making one policy safe across the whole map.
\item \textbf{Demand-aware buffer sizing} (B from measured demand minus surviving capacity
--- the urgent20 result shows the frozen urgent0-scaled grid undersizes when demand grows).
\item \textbf{Recurring-disruption axis} (new regime --- your call): standing insurance
amortizes across events; a single-event episode understates it.
\item \textbf{For the eventual corrected DRL:} the bar is the compound, per regime ---
273k (slack) / 767k--1,865k (sat50, by config) --- same seeds, same gates, both accounting
conventions, fairness-preserving. Given items 1--3, the honest expectation remains that
simple rules saturate this environment; the DRL comparison should be framed as measuring
\emph{how close} learning comes to the structured optimum, not whether it ``wins.''
\item \textbf{Paper framing} (consistent with the advisor's decision-first agenda): a
value-of-information and value-of-structure decomposition with a lever-flip map, in which
learned policies have measured, near-zero residual room --- a cost-of-AI result grounded
entirely in audited, deterministic experiments.
\end{enumerate}
